# Submission-ready checklist

Do not label a manuscript submission-ready until every critical item is present or explicitly confirmed not applicable.

## Scientific integrity

- [ ] Central question and contribution are explicit.
- [ ] Every major claim maps to supplied results.
- [ ] Text, tables, figures, legends, and supplement use consistent values.
- [ ] Association and causation language matches study design.
- [ ] Null findings and limitations needed for interpretation are reported.
- [ ] All citations are supplied or verified.

## Methods and microbiome reporting

- [ ] Study groups, analyzed sample sizes, exclusions, and endpoints are clear.
- [ ] Collection, storage, extraction, assay/sequencing, and controls are described.
- [ ] Bioinformatics software, versions, databases, and key parameters are described.
- [ ] Statistical tests, covariates, repeated measures, multiple testing, and uncertainty are described.
- [ ] Data and code availability are accurately stated.

## Declarations

- [ ] Ethics and consent/animal approval are present when required.
- [ ] Author contributions are confirmed by the authors.
- [ ] Funding and disclosure statements are confirmed.
- [ ] Corresponding-author details are complete.
- [ ] Acknowledgments are confirmed.

## Manuscript package

- [ ] Article type and current official instructions were checked.
- [ ] Title, abstract, keywords, main text, references, and legends are complete.
- [ ] Every figure/table is cited in order and has a self-contained legend/title.
- [ ] Supplementary items are cited and named consistently.
- [ ] Genus/species formatting, abbreviations, units, and statistical symbols are consistent.
- [ ] No author-input placeholders remain.

## Word QA

- [ ] Real Word heading and list styles are used.
- [ ] Page and line numbering meet current instructions or author preference.
- [ ] All pages were rendered and visually inspected.
- [ ] No clipping, overlap, broken tables, missing glyphs, or orphaned captions remain.

If any critical checkbox fails, deliver a draft and explain the blocker.
